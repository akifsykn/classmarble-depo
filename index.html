Before you order

The questions that come first

Can I have a piece made to my own size?

Yes. Custom sizes and designs are what the workshop specialises in. You can request specific sizes, finishes and designs for your project, for both basins and shower niches.

Will I pay customs charges in the US?

No. All US orders ship DDP (Delivered Duty Paid). Customs duties and import taxes are covered, so nothing extra is due on delivery.

How long does it take?

Production usually takes 5–10 business days, followed by fast worldwide shipping. Delivery time then depends on your location.

Will my piece look exactly like the photograph?

Close, but not identical. Because every piece is cut from natural stone, colour and veining vary slightly from one to the next. That variation is the character of the material rather than a fault.

Do you work with designers and contractors?

Yes. The workshop works with interior designers, contractors and businesses on wholesale pricing and project-based orders.

How is it packed, and what if it arrives damaged?

Every piece is inspected, then packed in a secure wooden crate. If something arrives damaged, send clear photographs of the product and packaging within 48 hours and it will be resolved.
