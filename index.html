What people ask before they spend $600 on stone

These pieces are made to order and cannot be returned, so nothing below is left vague. If your question is not here, message the workshop on Etsy and you will get a direct answer from Abdülkadir.

Can you make it in my size?

Yes, on every piece in the shop. We can produce in any size you need. Send the width, height and depth of the opening, or a photo of the space with measurements marked, and we cut to it. This is the request we receive most often and the one buyers mention most in their reviews.

Will I be charged customs when it arrives?

Not in the United States. All US orders ship DDP, delivered duty paid. We cover the customs duties and import taxes, so there is nothing further to pay at the door.

How is stone shipped without breaking?

In a secure wooden crate built around the piece. If something does arrive damaged, contact us within 48 hours with clear photos of both the product and the packaging and we will resolve it.

Will it look exactly like the photograph?

The form will. The veining will not, and that is the point. Since we use natural stone, each piece is unique, with slight variations in colour and veining from block to block. You are buying one cut of a real quarry block, not a printed surface.

Do you supply trade and wholesale?

Yes. We work with interior designers, contractors and businesses. Contact us for wholesale pricing and project-based orders — multiple bathrooms, a hotel floor, or a repeat specification across a development.

How long until it is on site?

Production usually takes 5 to 10 business days from the moment the size is confirmed, followed by worldwide shipping. Tell us your site date before you order and we will tell you honestly whether we can meet it.

From your measurement to the wall

The order is placed and paid on Etsy. Everything before and after it happens in messages with the workshop.

Before you order

Send the opening

Message the shop with the width, height and depth you need, and the stone you want it cut from. We confirm what is possible in that block before you pay anything.

Day 1

The block is selected

We choose the slab and mark the cut so the veining falls where you want it. On a basin, that usually means running the strongest vein across the front face.

Days 1–10

Carved by hand

Production takes 5 to 10 business days. The bowl, the recess and the edges are worked by hand, then the surface is finished and sealed.

Then

Crated and shipped

The piece is packed in a wooden crate and shipped worldwide. US orders travel DDP, so duties and import taxes are already settled when it lands.

Specifying stone for a project

The shop listings are sized for one bathroom. If you are buying for more than one, the workshop quotes it directly.
